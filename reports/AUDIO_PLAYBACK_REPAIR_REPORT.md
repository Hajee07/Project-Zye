### 2. Failure Analysis & Resolution

1. **Premature Completion Signaling**:
   - *Previous behavior*: Calling `set_state(LiveSessionState.LISTENING)` when server sends `turn_complete`, while buffer has 2.5 seconds of unplayed audio.
   - *Fix*: Implement `await player.wait_for_playback_complete(timeout=10.0)` before resetting state to `LISTENING`.

2. **Acoustic Bleed Prevention (Self-Listening Suppression)**:
   - *Previous behavior*: Gating checked only `self.player.is_playing()`, ignoring external Pygame playback from `main.py`.
   - *Fix*: Provide global playback check `is_any_playback_active()` combining `LiveAudioPlayer.is_playing()` and Pygame `pygame.mixer.music.get_busy()`.

3. **Safe Barge-In Handling**:
   - *Previous behavior*: High sensitivity to room acoustics caused false barge-in during loud playback sections.
   - *Fix*: During active playback, genuine barge-in requires user speech RMS $> 4,500$ sustained over at least 4 frames (120ms).
   - When genuine barge-in is detected, flush playback queues, stop Pygame, clear sounddevice buffers, and immediately switch to `USER_SPEAKING`.

4. **Preservation of User Voice Identity**:
   - The user's preferred Indian voice (`en-IN-NeerjaNeural` via Edge-TTS) is strictly preserved. Text responses generated by Gemini Live or Gemini Gateway pass through `UnifiedOutputCoordinator` and Edge-TTS with identical rate, pitch, and volume parameters.
